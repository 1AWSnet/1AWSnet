About

1AWS.net is run by Julio Rodriguez, a hands-on systems designer and network administrator with more than a decade of experience building networks, from server racks to rooftop radios.

Why 1AWS.net

  • Design to install: The person who plans your system is the one who installs and supports it.
  • Security built in: Firewalls, VPNs, backups and staff training are part of the plan, not an afterthought.
  • Open 7 days a week: 9 AM to 9 PM, with overnight work available when scheduled.
  • Clear pricing: $50 per hour, 2-hour minimum. Consultations and quotes are free.
