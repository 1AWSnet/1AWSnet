Camera styles

  • Bullet: Long, tube-shaped cameras for walls and outdoor areas. Easy to aim at a specific spot and a visible deterrent.
  • Dome: The lens sits under a clear or tinted cover. Vandal-resistant, and it's hard to tell which way it's pointing. A good fit for hallways, stores and public areas.
  • Turret: Also called eyeball cameras. The lens is exposed, with no cover to reflect infrared light, so night images are usually clearer.
  • PTZ: Pan, tilt and zoom. One camera can follow activity or cover a large area like a parking lot or yard.
  • Fisheye or 360° panoramic: One camera covers an entire room from the ceiling. Useful for open offices, warehouses and retail floors.
  • Dual-lens: Two lenses in one housing to cover a wide area, or a wide view and a zoomed view at the same time.
  • ALPR: Automatic license plate recognition. Reads and logs plates at entrances, gates and parking areas.
  • Video doorbells: Camera, intercom and doorbell in one, for front doors and deliveries.
