FAQ

How much does 1AWS.net charge?

Jobs are normally quoted at $75 per hour, with a 2-hour minimum on all jobs. For larger jobs, the rate can be as low as $50 per hour. Discounted packages of hours are coming soon.

Are consultations and quotes free?

Yes. Both are free, for every service.

What areas does 1AWS.net serve?

1AWS.net is based in Hartford, CT, and provides on-site work throughout New England and New York. Online consultations are available on the client's preferred video call service, and many requests can be handled through remote assistance.

What are 1AWS.net's hours?

9 AM to 9 PM, 7 days a week, including holidays. Overnight work (9 PM to 9 AM) is available when it's scheduled during standard hours.

What services does 1AWS.net offer?

Networking and security, virtualization, backups and disaster recovery, VoIP phone systems, camera systems, software and web development, automation, business IT support and security awareness training. See the full list on the Services page.

Can 1AWS.net help remotely?

Yes. Remote assistance is part of Business IT Support, and many requests can be handled without a site visit.

Does 1AWS.net train employees on security?

Yes. OpSec & Security Awareness Training takes 45 to 60 minutes, on-site for up to 20 employees or remote for up to 10. See what it covers.

How do I get started?

Call 860-249-1144 or email contact@1aws.net to set up a free consultation.
